video_pub

A personal publishing automation tool — pushes pre-edited educational video clips to the operator's own social accounts via each platform's official API.

What it does

video_pub is operated by a single creator (SuperVictor) to publish their own Linux/networking tutorial clips to their own accounts on YouTube and TikTok. It is a backend script, not a public service — it has no sign-up flow and no other users.

  • Authenticates once per platform via that platform's own OAuth flow.
  • Uploads a video file and caption that the operator has already produced and reviewed.
  • Confirms the publish completed successfully.
